# Problem Statement

## Official Problem

Financial institutions detect millions of fraud signals daily, but investigation workflows remain manual, slow, and expensive.

Compliance teams must analyze contextual data, justify decisions, and maintain documentation.

## Problem A — Detection

Existing banking systems and fraud models already generate suspicious transaction alerts.

## Problem B — Investigation

After an alert is generated, analysts must:

* collect information
* inspect transaction history
* examine customer context
* check devices
* investigate beneficiaries
* check sanctions/watchlists
* investigate network relationships
* assess risk
* prepare documentation

## Core Gap

Sentinel AI focuses on the investigation stage rather than replacing the initial transaction-detection systems.

## Consequences

### Analysts

Large amounts of time are spent on repetitive evidence collection and report preparation.

### Banks

Manual investigation creates operational cost and investigation backlogs.

### Customers

Legitimate transactions may experience delays when investigation capacity is limited.

### Regulators

Inconsistent investigation documentation makes compliance oversight more difficult.

## Sentinel AI Response

Automate evidence collection, network investigation, risk assessment, and report preparation while retaining analyst review and auditability.
